# MARKETING_PROFITABILITY
## Business Insights, Strategic Recommendations & Action Plan

### 1. Executive Overview

This report presents actionable business recommendations based on the Marketing Campaign Profitability Analysis & Budget Optimization project.

The analysis evaluates 120 campaigns across five advertising channels using Excel, MySQL, and Python.

The objective is to identify opportunities to improve marketing efficiency, reduce acquisition costs, and strengthen advertising profitability through data-driven decisions.

### 4. Recommendation 1: Evaluate Affiliate Budget Expansion

**Observation**

Affiliate generated the highest historical ROAS at 6.54x and the lowest advertising cost per conversion at 82.87.

It also generated approximately 4.75 million in marketing profit.

**Recommended Action**

Evaluate controlled increases in Affiliate spending, prioritizing campaigns with strong conversion quality and consistent financial performance.

**Implementation Steps**

1. Identify high-performing Affiliate campaigns using campaign-level ROAS and marketing profit.
2. Review available audience reach and partner capacity.
3. Test a limited incremental budget increase.
4. Monitor cost per conversion, attributed revenue, and incremental ROAS.
5. Expand investment only if additional spending meets profitability targets.

**Expected Business Benefit**

Potential improvement in marketing returns through increased investment in historically efficient campaigns.

### 5. Recommendation 2: Strengthen Paid Search Investment

**Observation**

Paid Search achieved:

- ROAS of 6.06x.
- Highest CTR at 3.49%.
- Highest lead conversion rate at 15.35%.
- Advertising cost per conversion of 89.54.

**Recommended Action**

Prioritize high-performing Paid Search campaigns for controlled testing and optimization.

**Implementation Steps**

1. Identify campaigns with strong conversion rates and ROAS.
2. Review keyword relevance and search intent.
3. Optimize advertisements and landing pages.
4. Test incremental budget increases.
5. Monitor changes in cost per conversion and marginal returns.

**Expected Business Benefit**

Potential growth in qualified traffic, conversions, and attributed revenue.

### 6. Recommendation 3: Optimize Paid Social Campaigns

**Observation**

Paid Social generated approximately 4.69 million in attributed revenue and achieved 4.76x ROAS.

Its acquisition efficiency was weaker than Affiliate and Paid Search.

**Recommended Action**

Maintain investment while improving campaign targeting, creative effectiveness, and conversion performance.

**Implementation Steps**

1. Compare performance across Paid Social campaigns.
2. Identify campaigns with high spending and lower ROAS.
3. Test alternative audience segments.
4. Experiment with advertisement creatives.
5. Review landing-page conversion performance.
6. Reallocate spending within Paid Social based on tested results.

**Expected Business Benefit**

Improved campaign efficiency without necessarily increasing the total advertising budget.

### 7. Recommendation 4: Improve Display Advertising Efficiency

**Observation**

Display recorded:

- ROAS of 3.91x.
- Lowest CTR at 0.60%.
- Advertising cost per conversion of 141.65.

Although Display generated positive marketing profit, its efficiency was below the three strongest channels.

**Recommended Action**

Review Display campaign targeting, placements, and conversion quality.

**Implementation Steps**

1. Identify campaigns with weak engagement and low ROAS.
2. Review placement quality and audience relevance.
3. Test improved advertising creatives.
4. Evaluate remarketing opportunities.
5. Pause or redesign persistently inefficient campaigns after controlled evaluation.

**Expected Business Benefit**

Potential reduction in inefficient advertising expenditure and improved returns.

### 8. Recommendation 5: Investigate Video Advertising Performance

**Observation**

Video achieved the lowest historical ROAS at 2.52x and the highest advertising cost per conversion at 210.55.

**Recommended Action**

Conduct a detailed review before reducing Video advertising expenditure.

**Implementation Steps**

1. Identify Video campaign objectives.
2. Separate direct-response campaigns from brand-awareness campaigns.
3. Review audience targeting and creative performance.
4. Examine assisted conversions where reliable attribution data is available.
5. Test a limited budget reduction for campaigns with weak incremental performance.
6. Monitor the impact on overall marketing outcomes.

**Expected Business Benefit**

Better alignment between Video spending, campaign objectives, and measurable business value.

A low direct-response ROAS does not automatically mean a brand-awareness campaign is ineffective.

### 9. Recommendation 6: Test Budget Reallocation

**Observation**

Affiliate and Paid Search historically generated stronger advertising returns than Video.

**Illustrative Scenario**

Reduce Video spending by 10% and reallocate the released budget:

| Channel | Budget Change |
|---|---:|
| Video | -82,809.45 |
| Affiliate | +49,685.67 |
| Paid Search | +33,123.78 |
| Total Change | 0.00 |

The scenario preserves the total advertising budget.

**Recommended Action**

Use the scenario as a hypothesis for a controlled experiment rather than implementing the full reallocation immediately.

**Implementation Steps**

1. Select eligible campaigns for a limited test.
2. Establish baseline conversion and revenue metrics.
3. Apply a controlled budget adjustment.
4. Measure incremental conversions and revenue.
5. Compare observed performance against the scenario assumptions.
6. Expand or reverse the adjustment based on measured results.

**Expected Business Benefit**

A better understanding of whether alternative budget allocations can improve actual incremental returns.

Monitoring frequency should be adjusted based on campaign volume, conversion lag, and business requirements.

### 12. Risks and Mitigation Strategies

| Risk | Potential Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Audience saturation | Reduced returns as spending increases | Scale budgets gradually |
| Attribution limitations | Misleading channel comparisons | Validate attribution assumptions |
| Seasonal demand changes | Unstable campaign performance | Compare appropriate time periods |
| Rising advertising costs | Higher acquisition costs | Monitor CPC and cost per conversion |
| Brand-awareness effects | Undervaluation of upper-funnel channels | Evaluate campaign objectives |
| Incomplete cost accounting | Overstatement of financial profitability | Include non-advertising costs when available |
| Conversion duplication | Overstatement of customer acquisition | Distinguish conversion events from unique customers |

### 13. Success Measurement

The proposed optimization initiatives should be evaluated against agreed business targets.

Recommended success criteria include:

- Improved incremental ROAS.
- Stable or reduced advertising cost per conversion.
- Increased qualified conversions.
- Improved marketing profit after accounting for relevant costs.
- Consistent performance across multiple measurement periods.
- No material deterioration in important brand or upper-funnel outcomes.

Numerical targets should be established before experimentation rather than selected after observing results.

### 15. Final Business Conclusion

The analysis identifies Affiliate and Paid Search as the strongest channels based on historical ROAS and acquisition efficiency.

Paid Social presents opportunities for continued optimization, while Display and Video require additional investigation into advertising efficiency and campaign objectives.

The recommended strategy is to prioritize measured incremental returns, controlled experimentation, and consistent KPI monitoring.

Rather than reallocating budgets solely on historical ROAS, marketing teams should combine profitability analysis with business constraints, campaign objectives, and reliable evidence of incremental performance.

This approach supports more informed, transparent, and financially disciplined marketing decisions.
